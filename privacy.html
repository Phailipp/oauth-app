Privacy Policy

Mail Tools is used only by its two owners, Philipp and Mariella Preisinger, for their own Gmail accounts and only with their own consent.

The app reads, searches and drafts emails in these accounts. The owners use the data only for their own email work, partly with an AI assistant they run themselves. The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
